## Project Overview

The **Swift Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard** is a data analytics project designed to monitor and evaluate sales performance across different regions, product categories, customer segments, and payment methods.

The dashboard provides business insights into:

* Revenue performance
* Product sales trends
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Regional performance
* Returned products analysis
* Payment method distribution
* Order fulfillment status

The project was built using spreadsheet-based analytics and dashboard visualization techniques.

# Key Insights from the Dashboard

## Revenue Trends

* Electronics generated the highest revenue contribution.
* South South region recorded the highest sales revenue.
* Monthly revenue showed fluctuations with peak performance toward the end of the year.

---

## Customer Behavior

* Returning customers contributed significantly to total revenue.
* Adult customers represented the largest purchasing age group.
* Female customers generated slightly higher revenue than male customers.

---

## Product Insights

* Laptops and smartphones ranked among the top-performing products.
* Fashion items generated lower revenue compared to electronics.

---

## Operational Findings

* Delivered orders accounted for the majority of transactions.
* Returned orders remained relatively low compared to completed orders.
* Card payments were the most frequently used payment method.
